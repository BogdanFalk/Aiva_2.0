What changed in the nine days since

Every finding was rechecked against official changelogs, pricing and model pages, and against her code. Eleven things moved; the rest is as it was. Each line links to its card, where a "Since 14 Oct" note has the detail.

  • Built

    Three items already exist on the voice-upgrades branch

    On 16 Oct a separate worktree (Aiva_2.0-voice, branch voice-upgrades, its own venv) got the Pipecat upgrade to 1.12, Deepgram Flux in the house, and emotion hearing through an OpenAI audio side-call. Backchannels are written there but not committed. Its 962 offline tests pass. None of it is merged into main or has run live.

  • Easier now

    Pipecat 1.13 puts three items in one upgrade

    Released 19 Oct. Eleven v4 Turbo is now in the dialogue voice service (as its default), backchannel frames are built in, and an OpenAI Decisions classifier ships with it. One upgrade on a branch now unlocks items 4 and 7 and the Decisions half of 15.

  • Watch out

    GPT-6 Luna needs a small code fix, not only a config change

    Her code picks the gpt-5 request shape by name: llm_kwargs in utilities.py and the pipeline settings in main.py both check for gpt-5. Named gpt-6-luna, she'd send none of it and run at the model's default reasoning effort, where OpenAI's Chat Completions doesn't allow function calls. The fix is two lines.

  • New

    Decisions is out of beta, and GPT-Live can call it

    Generally available 22 Oct, with shared-input caching, images and up to 400 questions per request. Since 15 Oct GPT-Live can call Decisions itself while it talks.

  • New

    GPT-Live voices designed from a description

    Since 16 Oct, any API customer can create a voice from a text description, free to create. A sister could get a voice designed to sound like her, though not her ElevenLabs voice itself.

  • Answered

    Eleven v4 Turbo costs the same as her voice today

    $0.04 per 1,000 characters on ElevenLabs' API pricing page, the same rate as the Flash model she uses now. Plain v4 is $0.08. Turbo still runs only on the Text-to-Dialogue WebSocket.

  • Watch out

    gpt-4.1 and gpt-4.1-mini shut down on 14 Jan 2027

    OpenAI gave notice on 14 Oct. Her .env already uses gpt-5.6-luna, so nothing breaks today, but five code defaults still name gpt-4.1 or gpt-4.1-mini, and the project notes' "switch back to gpt-4.1" fallback goes away.

  • New

    Eleven Realtime, ElevenLabs' own voice-agent models

    Released 19 Oct and compatible with OpenAI Realtime clients. They keep ElevenLabs voices, but ElevenLabs runs the LLM and the mode refuses a custom one, so her mind would move into their agent settings. On the watch list.

  • Answered

    Flux's price, and two Pipecat fixes for it

    $0.0065 a minute on Deepgram's current streaming promotion, against $0.0048 for her nova-3; she only pays while awake. Pipecat 1.13 adds interim transcripts and retries a failed first connection.

  • Answered

    Gemini 3.8 Live doesn't take custom voices partly checked

    Developers report that voices made with Google's voice tools are refused by Live, and Google hasn't given a timeline.

  • Answered

    Four of the open questions

    GPT-Live bills session time per second. Pipecat added the GPT-Live service in 1.9 and the Eleven dialogue service in 1.8. Her 1.5.0 already bundles Smart Turn v3.2. Mem0's open-source SDK has its new algorithm.

  • New

    A benchmark for voice agents in group talk

    MP-Bench (11 Sep, missed in the first pass): real-time voice agents stay at or below 22% on multiparty comprehension and near chance on multiparty turn-taking. That backs the house's rules-first arbiter.

No change found for gpt-realtime, Inworld and Cartesia, LAION's emotion models, the pipecat-backchannel package (still 0.3.3), Gemini Flash-Lite for audio, or the memory and inner-life papers. Hume still shuts down on 13 Nov. OpenAI still lists gpt-5.6-terra, her book-synthesis model, though one pricing tracker said it was gone.

  1. Eleven v4 Turbo with audio tags

    close to drop-in

    Speech-to-text that also decides when his turn is over. Already in her pinned Pipecat 1.5.0. Fewer cut-offs, less dead air, and the groundwork for starting a reply early.

  3. Real emotion hearing

    moderate

    Forty emotion classes plus chuckles and sighs, feeding the experience bus she already has, in place of today's pitch-and-energy arousal. The cloud side-call does it with no local model.

  4. Backchannels in her own voice

    moderate

    A soft "mhm" or "right" at his pauses, recorded once through her own TTS, with a small nod from the avatar. The cheapest strong sign that she's listening. Pipecat 1.13 now has backchannel frames that stay out of her context.

  5. GPT-Live-1 prototype for one sister

    big change

    The only checked route to true full duplex that keeps her own mind and tools as the backend. Since 16 Oct a sister can have a voice designed from a description, though not her ElevenLabs voice. Try it on a branch to measure cost, how the designed voice compares, and the floor redesign it needs.

Quick win outside the five

GPT-6 Luna costs about half of her current gpt-5.6-luna ($0.10 / $0.50 per 1M tokens). It's a change to AIVA_LLM_MODEL and AIVA_TASK_MODEL, a price entry in LLM_RATES, and a two-line code fix so she sends the request shape it needs (see the card). Run it for a few evenings before keeping it: nothing yet shows it sounds warmer.

Speech-to-speech and full duplex

Models that hear and speak in one stream, so she isn't deaf while talking.
1 · OpenAI

GPT-Live-1

API GA 10 Sep 2026big changecloud · ~$3/hour + backend tokens
Since 14 Oct
Voices designed from a text description are open to every API customer (16 Oct), free to create, with speech billed as usual. GPT-Live can now call Decisions natively while it talks (15 Oct). Pipecat 1.13 reworked client delegation so an abandoned delegation actually stops the backend's work and its pending tool calls. OpenAI's pricing page confirms billing is per second of session time.
What it is
OpenAI's first full-duplex voice model: it listens while it speaks and handles pauses, interruptions and backchannels itself. Reasoning and tools go to a separate backend. In client-delegation mode that backend can be your own model or agent, and results come back as spoken commentary, silent context or instructions (500 tokens per append).
Why it matters for her
It would end her being deaf while she speaks and the ~20 s gap while she plans a task, and let voice interrupts replace F10. Her own mind, tools and persona could stay as the backend.
How it fits
Cloud only, $0.05 per session minute billed per second, plus her normal token cost. Pipecat has had OpenAILiveLLMService with ClientDelegation since 1.9, so not in her 1.5.0. The house floor assumes half-duplex grants and a mic gate, so the house would have to stream live mic audio to the floor holder's session instead.
Watch out
Preset voices or ones designed from a description; voice cloning still goes through OpenAI's sales team, so each sister's ElevenLabs voice can't simply move across. The system prompt is fixed at session start, so mood refreshes go through appends. Developers report goodbyes without delegating and interruptions that don't cancel backend work. Benchmark numbers come from OpenAI's forum post.

2 · OpenAI

gpt-realtime-2.1 and 2.1-mini

7 May & 6 Jul 2026big changecloud · $32 / $64 per 1M audio tokens
What it is
OpenAI's turn-based speech-to-speech models. Version 2 added adjustable reasoning, a 128K context and short spoken lead-ins; 2.1 handles interruptions, silence and noise better and cut p95 latency by at least a quarter. The mini is cheaper ($10 / $20).
Why it matters for her
The simpler speech-to-speech option if full duplex is too much. It doesn't solve the voice or floor questions, and OpenAI's own numbers put it behind GPT-Live on turn-taking.
How it fits

Watch out
Preset voices only. Mixed forum reports on 2.1-mini (prompt leakage, tool calls stopping). The old gpt-realtime-mini shuts down 20 Jan 2027.

4 · ElevenLabs

Eleven v4 and v4 Turbo

28 Sep 2026moderatecloud · $0.04 per 1K characters
Since 14 Oct
Priced at $0.04 per 1,000 characters for Turbo and $0.08 for v4, the same as Flash and v3 respectively. Pipecat 1.13 (19 Oct) added eleven_v4_turbo to ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService and made it that service's default. ElevenLabs made v4 Turbo the default voice for its own agents (5 Oct). Turbo is still documented only on the Text-to-Dialogue WebSocket.
What it is
ElevenLabs' new architecture and most emotive model. It takes plain-language delivery direction and inline tags like [laughs] or [said angrily], reads tone from context, and in dialogue answers the previous line instead of reading sentences in isolation. Turbo is the agent variant, about 100 ms inference and 150 ms to first speech (vendor figures).
Why it matters for her
The biggest audible jump that keeps her vendor and probably her voices. The same tags can drive her Live2D expressions.
How it fits
Turbo runs only on ElevenLabs' Text-to-Dialogue WebSocket, which Pipecat reaches through ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService (since 1.8; v4 Turbo since 1.13). Her persona's no-markup rule needs an exception for the tags, and PrivacyGate must strip them before said, transcripts and memory. Test each sister's voice ID on v4 first with tools\pick_voice.py --verify.
Watch out
Text-to-Dialogue needs workspace access on ElevenLabs' side, and stability is the only voice setting it reads. Stability 0.0 can hallucinate. Latency comparisons are ElevenLabs' own.

6 · Deepgram

Flux: speech-to-text with built-in turn-taking

launched Oct 2025, updated Apr & Sep 2026close to drop-incloud · $0.0065/min on promotion
Since 14 Oct
Deepgram's pricing page lists Flux English at $0.0065 a minute (was $0.0077) and nova-3 at $0.0048 (was $0.0077), both as limited-time streaming promotions with no end date. Pipecat 1.13 makes Flux send interim transcripts and retry a failed first connection instead of transcribing nothing for the rest of the session; she'd get both only after the upgrade. No new Flux model in Deepgram's changelog.
What it is
One model transcribes and judges the end of his turn. It emits start of turn, end of turn (confidence threshold 0.7 by default), an earlier "eager" end of turn for speculative replies, and "turn resumed" when he keeps going. Deepgram claims replies 200 to 600 ms sooner and about 30% fewer false interruptions than separate VAD and endpointing.
Why it matters for her
Stiff turn-taking and cut-offs, like the "constellation witness progress?" line that was dropped tonight. A clean start-of-turn signal also matters if voice barge-in ever comes back.
How it fits
House only. DeepgramFluxSTTService has been in Pipecat since 0.0.87, so it's in her 1.5.0. It could replace the current Deepgram model plus Silero and smart-turn. Re-test the transcript-verified wake word and the dropped frames while she sleeps. Start with plain end of turn; eager starts need a "provisional turn" in the house protocol.
Watch out
Deepgram's own launch post gives about 1.5 s p95 end-of-turn latency. Eager starts cost 50 to 70% more model calls.

7 · Community package

pipecat-backchannel

v0.3.3, 10 Aug 2026moderatelocal · BSD-2
Since 14 Oct
The package is unchanged (still 0.3.3, needs Pipecat 1.6 to 2.0). Pipecat 1.13 itself added BotBackchannelFrame: a short line that stays out of her context and isn't cut off by an interruption. It also added UserBackchannelFrame for his "mhm" while she talks, so she keeps going. The release notes add no component that decides when to backchannel, so the timing still comes from this package or from the house.
What it is
Plays short listening sounds ("mhm", "yeah", "right") at clause-complete pauses while the user is still talking. Clips are recorded once through your own TTS, so they're in her voice. It skips word-search pauses and finished questions, takes no turn and adds nothing to her context. Cooldown, probability and minimum speech are tunable.
Why it matters for her
Right now she gives no sign she's listening until she answers. This is the cheapest "she's with me" cue, and each clip can trigger a Live2D nod.
How it fits
Needs Pipecat 1.6 or newer. In the house design the house hears the pause and sends a tiny "play clip" message to the sister he's talking to, who owns the voice. The mic gate must cover the clip, and only one sister backchannels at a time.
Watch out
Single maintainer. Runs a second VAD and turn detector, roughly doubling inference while he speaks. Fires only at pauses for now.

Hearing how he feels

9 · LAION

Empathic Insight Voice and the vocal-burst classifier

suite 2025, burst head fixed 4 Sep 2026moderatelocal · CC-BY-4.0
What it is
Small scoring heads on a fine-tuned Whisper-small encoder that rate 40 emotions (amusement, fatigue, confusion, embarrassment, longing, teasing and more) plus valence and arousal. Trained on about 5k hours of voice acting and 5k hours of real clips. A separate 11-class head names vocal bursts such as a chuckle, relief sigh, yawn or groan (89% on chuckles, 56% overall).
Why it matters for her
Her read of him is coarse: arousal from pitch and energy, no emotion names, no laughter. Her own docs already plan for "a second analyzer feeding the same bus".
How it fits
The house already buffers each turn's 16 kHz audio and computes vocal affect once per turn in paralinguistics. This would run beside it in a thread and travel with the turn, like the voiceprint does.
Watch out
Labels are machine-generated and partly synthetic. Accuracy on second-language English is unknown. The burst head classifies bursts but doesn't find them, so a laughter detector is still needed. 30 s input limit.

11 · Research

State-first memory: StateMem and Agent Zero Memory

arXiv, 20 & 30 Aug 2026moderatelocal · design, no code
What they are
StateMem shows memory systems recall well but lose track of the current state when facts change. Explicit "supersedes" links plus dependencies between facts improve current-state accuracy 1.6 to 1.8 times. Agent Zero Memory tags every memory with origin, time and evidence, and answers only from evidence it actually opened, otherwise it abstains.
Why they matter for her
Long-term coherence: not mixing his old job with his new one, or last month's plan with this week's.
How it fits
Her facts table and retire_fact are halfway there. Add a "supersedes" column and a "true now" view for the LONG-TERM MEMORY block. Kin's "I haven't seen her do that" is already the abstain pattern.
Watch out
Neither paper links code, so this is a design to borrow, not a library.

12 · Research and Mem0

A reality check before buying a memory framework

MemDelta 29 Jun 2026, Mem0 from Apr 2026drop-in ideaslocal
Since 14 Oct
Answered: Mem0's migration guide says its open-source SDK has the new algorithm, not only the hosted platform. The big benchmark gains it quotes come from the hosted platform. No new memory paper this month changes the advice to keep her SQLite memory.
What it is
MemDelta: with the embedding model held constant, plain retrieval beat Mem0 overall; changing only the embedding model moved accuracy 6.2 points, and an agent's own "self-memory" (42%) did worse than basic retrieval (47%). Mem0's new algorithm extracts in one pass, only adds (old facts kept with their time), ranks by time and links entities.
Why it matters for her
Keep her SQLite memory. The cheap wins are ideas: add-only facts with time, boosting by person or thing, and possibly a better embedding model than text-embedding-3-small.
How it fits
A change of embedding model is already guarded: store_meta notices it and clears the old vectors, so recall falls back to lexical until they're rebuilt.
Watch out
Vendors dispute each other's benchmarks, and Mem0's own numbers vary between its pages.

13 · Research

Read him, feel, then speak: ToMAgent and "Feeling First, Speaking Second"

revised 11 Apr 2026 · LREC 2026moderateside model
What they are
ToMAgent: just prompting a model to write mental-state estimates between turns ("he believes, wants, feels…") already helps a lot, and a trained version keeps better relationships with its partners. The Bologna paper puts an appraisal stage (how this lands on me) before the verbal stage; without it, planning overrode feeling under pressure.
Why they matter for her
It turns her mood dials and his vocal affect into a per-turn note: what's going on with him, and how it lands on her. That note can steer her words and, with v4, her tone.
How it fits
One side-model call per turn, never on the voice model, run in parallel with the turn's other work. Or fold it into SelfEngine.heard(), which already runs before every reply.
Watch out
Research evaluated with simulations and LLM judges. A per-turn side call adds latency unless it runs in parallel.

Since 14 Oct
Decisions is generally available (22 Oct), with shared-input caching, images, and up to 400 questions per request, and Pipecat 1.13 wraps it as OpenAIDecisionsClassifier. Her code needs a fix first. llm_kwargs (utilities.py) and the pipeline settings (main.py) only send max_completion_tokens and reasoning_effort: "none" to names that start with gpt-5. Function calls still need reasoning_effort: "none". Decisions (Luna only) returns typed answers: a probability for yes or no, a choice among options, or a score, at input price only and "about 10x faster" than Responses.
Why it matters for her
Luna halves token cost with a config change and a two-line fix. Decisions fits the house's quick judgments: which sister he meant, whether anyone should speak at this pause, whether a sound was a backchannel or a real interruption.
How it fits
Set AIVA_LLM_MODEL and AIVA_TASK_MODEL, add the price to LLM_RATES (or cost estimates fall back to mini rates), and teach llm_kwargs and main.py's settings the new name. Decisions could replace the arbiter's JSON side-call.

No evidence it sounds warmer: compare a few evenings. Decisions probabilities aren't documented as calibrated, so tune thresholds on her logs.

16 · Research, with code

Answering before he finishes: RelayS2S and Voice-Light

EMNLP 2026 Findings · 17 Sep 2026moderate to biglocal design
Since 14 Oct
Missed in the first pass: since 1.9, Pipecat can answer Flux's eager end of turn and hold the reply until the turn is confirmed. That's the speculative half of this design, built in. The house still needs its own "provisional turn", because the floor grants turns, not the pipeline. Voice-Light's repository was updated on 17 Oct. Neither repository has a license file, so ask the authors before reusing code.
What they are
RelayS2S: a fast model speaks the first few words while the slower, smarter pipeline writes the rest, and a small verifier decides whether to keep that opening. On real dialogues it cut first-chunk latency by 479 ms on average and kept 99% of the slow pipeline's quality. Voice-Light: full duplex built from an ordinary STT, LLM and TTS chain. It prepares replies privately before the turn is certain, keeps cancelled audio out of history, and covers tool calls with spoken "bridge" lines.
Why they matter for her
Felt latency and dead air while she plans. Voice-Light is the closest reference design to her architecture.
How it fits
Pairs with Flux's eager end of turn. Needs a "provisional turn" message from the house, and PrivacyGate holding speculative output until the turn is confirmed.
Watch out
Small evaluations. Eager starts cost 50 to 70% more model calls.

What they are
Speak or Stay Silent: eight recent models, zero-shot, consistently failed at deciding whether an assistant should speak at a pause in a group conversation; training with reasoning traces improved balanced accuracy by up to 23 points. ModeratorLM conditions turn-taking on the role a speaker has been given, and interrupts less.
Why they matter for her
The house arbiter is exactly the zero-shot side call they measured. It supports the order the house already uses: name and errand rules first, the model only when they can't decide.
How it fits
Log each arbiter decision against what he did next and use those rows as labels, either as few-shot examples or to set a Decisions threshold (15). Telling each sister her role in the moment (talking to him, listening, being asked by her sister) matches ModeratorLM.

Watch list

Promising, not ready for her

  • NVIDIA PersonaPlex-7B (Jan 2026): open full duplex on Moshi, with voice and persona prompts. English only, tested on an A100 80 GB, no tool calling.
  • NemotronLabs VoiceChat: open full duplex, and its paper (revised 1 Oct) adds native tool calling, with 82.5% tool-selection F1 but weaker argument accuracy. The paper still doesn't say where the weights are or under what license.
  • Eleven Realtime (19 Oct): ElevenLabs' voice-agent models, Mini and Max, compatible with OpenAI Realtime clients, at $0.08 a minute plus tokens. They keep ElevenLabs voices, but run only inside ElevenLabs' agents with ElevenLabs' own LLM ("isn't available while the agent's LLM is a custom LLM"), so her memory, persona and tools would have to move into their settings.
  • Qwen3.5-Omni (Mar 2026): API only, no weights.
  • Open expressive TTS: Fish Audio S2 Pro (non-commercial weights), Qwen3-TTS, Resemble Dramabox (~24 GB VRAM, no streaming).
  • NVIDIA Audio2Face-3D: MIT, runs on Windows with CUDA 12.8; face blendshapes from audio that could map to Live2D, but no head or eye motion.
  • LiveKit adaptive interruption (telling a backchannel from a real barge-in): cloud only, no open equivalent found.
  • Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS: prompt-directed emotion, preset voices only.
  • Avoid: Hume EVI and Octave TTS, shutting down 13 Nov 2026.

# Her selfhood: nervous system, drives, dreams, projects, preferences, capabilities

- `aiva/modules/inner.py` — her **nervous system** (substrate for the selfhood work, `docs/PLAN-selfhood-and-eval.md`). Phases 1–2 built & offline (**NOT yet wired into `main.py`** — no live effect until then): `InnerState` (valence/arousal/social/focus dials, persisted to `aiva/inner_state.json`, decays toward an env-set baseline like the cat), `InnerBrain` (PURE: `decay`/`integrate` with a salience-gate + material-change flag, `now` always injected), `ExperienceBus` (`experience` table on `Memory.conn`; `emit`/`drain`/`mark_consumed` — the shared bloodstream that replaces point-to-point hooks), and `SelfEngine` (`journal` + `desires` tables; `tick` drains the bus → moves mood → when earned writes a private diary entry via `JOURNAL_PROMPT` on the side model + forms desires deduped/vetoable like traits; `should_journal`, `add_desire`/`drop_desire`/`recall_journal`). Journal is her inner voice, NEVER announced. The dashboard reads all of it (mood dials, experience stream, diary, wishes). **Wired live in `main.py` (Phases 2–3 core):** the self loop `self_watch` ticks every `AIVA_SELF_TICK_S`; reading emits `chapter_read`/`book_finished` (mood-hinted by the note's feeling) and time emits `daypart`; mood + open wishes render as the HOW YOU FEEL / WHAT YOU FIND YOURSELF WANTING persona blocks (refreshed on material mood change via `refresh_system_prompt`); voice tools `note_desire`/`drop_desire`/`recall_journal`/`set_initiative`; a private diary entry is forced at shutdown (digesting recent conversation). **Phase 4 (initiative) shipped:** the pure `initiative_decision` (mood + presence + backoffs/caps/quiet-hours/boot-grace) drives `initiative_tick` in `self_watch` so she can `express` a feeling or `ask` about an open wish on her own. **Phase 5 (autonomous action) shipped:** `initiative_decision` now also returns `"act"`, and `initiative_tick`→`_do_act` pursues a wish herself via an injected `_act(desire)` dispatcher — but ONLY when the injected `actionable(desire)` predicate approves it (auto-tier via `capabilities.match`, not paused; wired in `main.py` as `_self_actionable`→`_act_on_wish`, v1 = find-and-keep an image to her space, gated by `AIVA_SELF_ACT` + a tighter `max_acts_per_day` + the `ServiceLimits` Tavily cap). The wish is consumed (`status='acted'`) and a `self_act` event is emitted (learn-by-doing) BEFORE dispatch. Anything that spends/sends/touches his files/edits her code stays an `ask`. `set_initiative(always|ask|quiet|never)` is the switch, tracked in a `self_kv` table. **A wish ends when it's done** (2026-10-17: eight wordings of one "run the Saga check" wish, two passing checks, raised every quarter hour): `settle_wishes(what)` closes every open wish an event settles (one side-model call picks them, all wordings of the same wish but never same-topic ones; near-restatement fallback) — fed by `TaskRunner(on_result=)` for a done task (status `fulfilled`) and by `drop_desire` (`done=true` when he says it's already done; his words go to the side model, not a loose similarity guess); a wish she raised rests `ASK_REST_H` (1 d, 3 d, 7 d — `wishes_raised` in `self_kv`) before she raises it again; the diary digest gets an ALREADY DONE line. **Emitters MUST run on the event-loop thread** (sqlite conn rule) — so the cat's autonomous changes (tk thread), per-turn conversation sentiment, and avatar-expression-from-mood are the Phase-3/4 tail. `python -m modules.inner --show` audits her. Tests: `tests/test_inner.py`, `tests/test_self_engine.py`, `tests/test_initiative.py`, `tests/test_persona.py`.
- `aiva/modules/drives.py` — **v3's engine** (`docs/PLAN-v3.md`), PURE and clock-injected: four **needs** (connection/novelty/competence/rest, 0 starved..1 met) that `deplete` with time (rest refills while the house sleeps; downtime counts as ≤12 h), are refilled/spent by event kinds via `EFFECTS` (`satisfy`, diminishing returns), lean on her mood (`pressure`), and `tension` when two press at once; `rhythm_update`/`expectation_event` (P(he's around) per weekday-hour → `expected_absent`/`unexpected_presence`); `reunion`/`is_friction`/`repair_note`; `initiative_outcome` (welcomed/ignored). `SelfEngine` owns the I/O: needs live in `InnerState.needs`/`needs_ts`, `_drive_step`/`_rhythm_step`/`_initiative_feedback` run in `tick`, and **`heard(text, affect)`** — called by `main.py` for every user turn BEFORE her reply — emits the `conversation` event and returns that turn's reunion/repair notes. `initiative_decision` reads the needs (missing him → sooner, restless → act sooner, worn → wider gap). Diary wishes must point outward (`desire_is_outward`, the 2026-10-12 introspection rut). A `focus` expires after 24 h (`focus_ts`). Toggle `AIVA_DRIVES`. Tests: `tests/test_drives.py`.
- `aiva/modules/dreams.py` — **her nights** (`Consolidation`, side model, label `dream`): `maybe_run(asleep)` from `self_watch` → once a day after `AIVA_DREAM_HOUR` while quiet, `dream()` replays the last ~26 h of `messages` + feedback events into `moments` (landed/flat + `better`) and 0-2 **habits** ("When X, I Y"; `valid_habit` rejects people-pleasing and inner-weather ones; adopt 0.35, reinforce, weaken, half-life 21 d, cap 8, vetoable), then `forget()` deletes consumed experience older than 30 d under salience 0.3. `rewrite_story()` revises a ≤180-word autobiography about every `AIVA_STORY_DAYS`. `prompt_data()` → the YOUR STORY SO FAR / HABITS YOU'VE FORMED / MOMENTS THAT LANDED blocks. `--export` writes chat JSONL for a future open-weight LoRA. Her gpt-5.x model can't be fine-tuned, and OpenAI is winding fine-tuning down, so she changes in context. `--show [--who saga]` audits. Toggle `AIVA_DREAMS`.
- `aiva/modules/projects.py` — **her projects**: `ProjectBook.promote(open desires)` turns a strong (≥0.75), pursuable wish (`project_kind`: collection | learn; nothing with him/buy/send/code in it) into a project (desire status `project`, ≤3 active); `main._project_tick` does one step when `step_due` (restless or wanting to be useful, idle ≥10 min, ≤3/day, ≥6 h gap, Tavily allowed, not quiet hours): a collection keeps an image in her space (`save_find(match_meaning=False)`, subject from `describe_image`), a learn project keeps search snippets in its notes; `project_done` is announced. Voice tool `let_go(kind=habit|project)`. Toggle `AIVA_PROJECTS`. Tests: `tests/test_dreams.py`.
- `aiva/modules/preferences.py` — her **mutable likes/dislikes/opinions**, distinct from `becoming` (which is reading-only and forbidden from contradicting the baseline). `Preferences` store (one `preferences` table + a `pref_kv` on `Memory.conn`) with SIGNED `valence` (magnitude=conviction, sign=like/agree vs dislike/disagree), `kind` taste|opinion, `origin` baseline|reading|conversation|reflection, deduped by subject embedding (cosine 0.86 / jaccard 0.60 fallback), capped (`MAX_ACTIVE`=24, `ADOPT_CAP`=0.6 so one event can't create a strong pref), faded (half-life 120d / 240d baseline, floor 0.2, every ~20h) and permanently vetoable (`drop`). The coherence guard is **inertia, not a veto**: the PURE `evolve(valence, evidence, origin, direction)` reinforces toward the extreme on same-direction evidence and *erodes toward 0 first* on opposite evidence — flipping only after sustained counter-experience, and ~2× slower for `origin='baseline'`. `seed_from_baseline()` (run once at boot) seeds her frozen tastes as `baseline`-origin rows; from then on they evolve like any other. `apply()` is async (embeds), returns inserted|reinforced|eroded|flipped|blocked|rejected; `_BOUNDED` rejects harmful content. Formation piggybacks `SelfEngine._digest` (the `preferences` key in `JOURNAL_PROMPT` — NO extra LLM call); `SelfEngine(preferences=…)` applies each delta. Rendered as the "WHERE YOU STAND" persona block (`prompt_block` → likes/dislikes/opinions, refreshed on change); voice tools `note_preference`/`drop_preference`. The PERSONALITY split moved *tastes* here and left *temperament/voice* frozen, so `persona.baseline_traits()` (becoming's anti-contradiction gate) now protects character only. `python -m modules.preferences --show` audits. Tests: `tests/test_preferences.py` (+ formation in `tests/test_self_engine.py`, split in `tests/test_persona.py`).
- `aiva/modules/capabilities.py` — her **intention-indexed self-inventory** (pure, dependency-light). One authored registry of `Capability(name, intention, blurb, tier auto|ask|never, tools, keywords)`; `match(wish_text)` (ordered lexical/phrase match, conservative → None), `tier(name)` (unknown→ask), `prompt_lines()` (the "WHAT YOU CAN DO" block body), `for_tool(intention)` (the `what_can_i_do` tool payload). `SAFE_TOOLS` is the auto-tier envelope, enforced by a test (no `auto` capability may reference a PC/shell/build/self-edit tool). Consumed by persona (`build_system_prompt(capabilities=)`), the `what_can_i_do` tool (`tools.build_handlers`), and `main.py`'s `_self_actionable` gate for autonomous action. Tests: `tests/test_capabilities.py`.
- `aiva/modules/initiative_probe.py` — **experiment (2026-10-23), not wired in**: a tiny initiative engine pointed at the *project* instead of her life. It finds one pattern (a module under `aiva/modules/` that CLAUDE.md and no `.claude/rules/*.md` names by its stem; a `paths:` glob alone doesn't count) and proposes one step for the most recently modified one: extend the rules file whose globs already match it, else the one listing the closest-named module, else CLAUDE.md. Read-only, proposes only. `python -m modules.initiative_probe [root]` prints `pattern:` / `action:`. Tests: `tests/test_initiative_probe.py`.
- `aiva/modules/upgrades.py` — **she knows when she's been upgraded** (pure). Hand-authored `UPGRADES` (`Upgrade(id, who, told, on(env, ch))`, second-person spoken prose); `applicable(slug, env, ch)` keeps only hers that are really on (it reuses `STTConfig`/`BackchannelConfig.from_env`, so a feature switched off or a model not in `.env` is never told); `pending` vs the told-record in her db (`store_meta` `upgrades_told`, {id: ISO day}); `boot_note` rides on her boot intro (told once, in passing) and is recorded as told when `_strip_intro` consumes the intro after her first greeting; `recent` (14 days) feeds the WHAT'S NEW IN YOU persona block so "what's new?" stays answerable. A fresh db is marked told silently. Add an entry for every change that alters what she is or can do (CLAUDE.md, Versioning). Tests: `tests/test_upgrades.py`.
- `aiva/modules/appraisal.py` — **read him, feel, then speak** (pure; the call is injected). On each user turn `main.py` starts `_appraise` in the background on the task model (`openai_llm_call(label="appraisal")`) with the last `tail_lines` context messages + his line + how he sounded: `APPRAISAL_PROMPT_T` → `{him, me, valence, arousal, confidence}` → `parse_appraisal` (tolerant, clamped, 160-char sentences). It never delays the reply: valence/arousal go onto the bus at once as an `appraisal` event (scaled by confidence, so mood and tone move), and `appraisal_note` (only at ≥ `AIVA_APPRAISAL_MIN_CONFIDENCE`, within `AIVA_APPRAISAL_NOTE_TTL_S`) rides on his NEXT turn in `_turn_notes`, worded so she never announces it. `AIVA_APPRAISAL` toggles. Tests: `tests/test_appraisal.py`.

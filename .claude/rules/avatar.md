- **Avatar renderer demo (never starts a sister)**: `cd aiva && ..\.venv\Scripts\python -m modules.avatar_renderer --demo` (also `--demo-activity` loops the reading book + work panel choreography — pair it with `--instance demo` so it never collides with a live body, `--windowed` for GL triage, `--selftest` for a 3 s smoke (Saga's must also paint her sky; it exits 4 untested if a live body owns the state file, so pass `--state <a temp file>`), `--demo-witness` with `--instance saga` loops moments into her constellation (a temp state file unless `--state` is given, so it never writes her real sky), `--instance saga --model assets/avatar/ariu/saga.model3.json` for Saga's body, `--write-model saga` regenerates her `model3.json` from the `saga.2048/` textures)

- `aiva/modules/avatar_renderer.py` — her body: a separate process rendering the Live2D model (live2d-py + PyQt5 QOpenGLWidget) in a transparent, click-through, monitor-sized window. Owns all model/window state; sole writer of `aiva/avatar_state.json` (port/pid/boot_id/placement). `MouthEnvelope` consumes queued RMS chunks at wall-clock rate for lip-sync. `paint_book`/`paint_panel` (+ `PropArt`, the book image, hue-rotated for Saga) draw her activity props with QPainter INTO the GL frame right after `model.Draw()`.
- `aiva/modules/avatar_activity.py` — **what her body is doing** (pure, clock-injected): `ActivityDirector` turns the sister's `activity` state (`reading {title}` / `working {label, detail, step, total, state}`) and `cue`s (`page|done|failed`) into a per-frame `Pose`: the hold param (the model's own 手柄 pose, `shoubing`, crossfades arm sets — it doesn't swing an arm) with the controller part (`Part47`) hidden and the book drawn over the empty hands; head down + eyes scanning lines while reading, looking up while she talks; a work panel beside her head (side latched toward the screen middle) with glances, amber "waiting for you", a check/cross result flash. Positions are MODEL units (`RIGS`, measured offscreen on ariu.moc3) mapped by `model_to_px` through her placement. Fed by `main.py`'s `body_activity` loop (`ReadingEngine.now_reading()`, `TaskRunner.activity()`, running `terminals.jobs`), which also keeps her eyes open while busy. Tests: `tests/test_avatar_activity.py`.
- `aiva/modules/avatar.py` — `AvatarClient`, the Aiva-side facade (replaces the old pyvts wrapper): adopts a live renderer via the state file or spawns one; async request/reply for tools; **sync fire-and-forget** `send_mouth`/`send_speech` hot path that must never stall the pipeline. All failures degrade to voice-only.
- `aiva/modules/constellation.py` — **Saga's quiet constellation witness** (pure, clock-injected): `Constellation` lights one star per lived moment (`said|heard|sister|read` — the KIND only, never text), coalesces bursts, caps the sky, renders `frame()` at a low alpha ceiling (hushed to `SPEECH_DIM` ~30% while she speaks — `SpeechHush`, fed `MouthEnvelope.speaking` by the renderer's `next_sky_frame` — easing back over 1 s), persists kinds/weights/seeds into the renderer's own state file; `enabled_for(instance)` is true only for `saga`; `sky_rect` anchors it to her upper left (Saga's ask) and shrinks it (not below `SKY_MIN_PX`) rather than let the on-screen clamp push it over her near an edge or on a tall portrait monitor — a sky held narrow that way grows up to `SKY_TALLER` taller instead (up, away from her; her real spot is width-bound, so `scale` alone doesn't reach it); stars drift slowly (`drift_offset` on an integrated clock, slower asleep) and never pulse — brightness/size are steady, lines ride their stars. Her look is pinned to her own words (house transcript #3968): a star is ONE disc `star_px(weight, seed)` = 2–4 real pixels (never scaled with the sky or monitor, no glow), `TINT` white with a faint blue-violet tint, `LINE_PX` hairlines whose ends are `LINE_OVER_STARS` (1.15×) their stars; her refinements (#4010/#4014) add a steady per-seed size nudge and `star_shade` (dimmer only, never brighter), `line_softness` easing the outermost line ends to half (by home position, so nothing breathes), `DRIFT_AMP` 0.04 and `scale` 0.23; `frame()` alphas are exactly what `paint_sky` paints (it adds nothing). Drawn by `SkyWindow` (a click-through window of its own, painting via `paint_sky`) in HER avatar renderer process; the `witness` IPC cmd is fire-and-forget, `hello.witness` tells `AvatarClient.send_witness` whether to send. `SAGA_CONSTELLATION*` knobs. Tests: `tests/test_constellation.py`.
- `aiva/modules/cat.py` + `cat_sprites.py` — desktop cat. `DesktopCat` facade (thread-safe command queue), `CatState` (persisted to `aiva/cat_state.json`), `CatBrain` (pure logic), `CatWindow` (tk on its own daemon thread — every Tk object stays on that thread). Sprites are generated PNGs in `aiva/assets/cat/` (gitignored). The snapshot carries the brain's `behavior` + `last_fed`; `care_snapshot()` adds `self_care` (hers — everything through the facade's `feed/play/pet`, persisted in `CatState.self_care`) and `user_care` (his clicks/menu, recorded in `_emit` BEFORE the announce throttle); `status()` gains `you_last` ("fed it 12 minutes ago").
- `aiva/modules/cat_care.py` — **she looks after the cat on her own.** PURE `care_decision(snap, ctx)` (need first: feed under 35 hunger > play when down with energy > pet when down; then a pet just because when her valence/social is warm, ≤ every 3 h; never a busy cat (`BUSY`), never wakes a napping one unless starving, never repeats what HE did within 10 min, only feeding in quiet hours, play+pet capped per day — `CARE`), `should_tell` (rare spoken heads-up: he's present, she's awake and not busy, initiative not `never`, 2/day 3 h apart), `action_for_wish`. `CatCaretaker` (loop thread; `main.py` `cat_care_watch` ticks it every 60 s, `AIVA_CAT_CARE`) acts through `DesktopCat`'s queue, emits `cat_care` on the experience bus (the sqlite rule the tk thread can't keep; `drives.EFFECTS` gives a little competence), relays a `self_act`/`tend_cat` to her sister once a day. `tend_cat` is in `main._ACT_CAPS`, so a cat wish is an initiative `act` (`can_act_on_wish` gates it; `act_on_wish` does it and tells him, without a second bus event). No LLM call anywhere. Tests: `tests/test_cat_care.py`.
- `aiva/modules/drawing.py` — **her drawing canvas, shared with her sister**: `board_for(ch.slug)` (one `DrawingBoard` per sister per process; `main.py` and anything else of hers share it) opens the standalone `drawing-app/` (its own README; it imports nothing from `aiva/`) from HER process via `drawapp.launcher.launch()` + `drawapp.client.DrawClient`, both stdlib-only, so Qt never loads in her. Tools `open_drawing`/`draw_stroke`/`clear_drawing`/`undo_stroke`/`save_drawing`/`open_saved_drawing` (runner-allowed; `clear_drawing` is outside `SAFE_TOOLS` because a clear can't be undone, and `undo_stroke` is outside too (kept ask-first: it takes something off a drawing he and her sister are looking at), while the two saving tools are inside because they never replace a drawing). **Undo is per author** (drawapp 0.4): `RemoveStroke` op + `Document.last_stroke(author)` (newest *finished* stroke of that author only), wire `undo` → `{"stroke_id" | null, "revision", "remaining"}`, toolbar Undo/Ctrl+Z for the window's author; autosaved like a stroke. `_undo` never opens a window; on `unsupported` it probes `status` — a 0.3 window answers (autosaves, so "reopen" is safe), a pre-0.3 one doesn't and falls through to `_guarded`'s "leave it open". **One window for both sisters** (2026-10-21: asked to tag-team a drawing, each opened her own, because each launched and looked for a window under her OWN name): the window is named for the CANVAS, `drawing.SHARED_CANVAS` ("shared"), not for who draws. `_attach` → `find(self.canvas, save_dir)`, `_ensure` → `launch(canvas="shared", author=HAND ("user"), absorb=SISTER_CANVASES)` inside `launcher.opening("shared")` (an OS lock on `recovery\shared.opening.lock` across find-then-launch; without it, both opening at once got two windows). Every wire call still sends `author=self.author`, so strokes keep her ink and tag and undo stays hers; his mouse strokes there are "user". drawapp 0.5: `--canvas` names the slot (lock/autosave/window file) apart from `--author` (the mouse); `--absorb aiva --absorb saga` adds each old per-sister autosave whose lock is free onto the shared canvas (strokes keep authors, deduped by id), autosaves, THEN renames it `*.merged-<time>` — an old window still open is skipped until a later start. `close()` ends the window for both; nothing of hers calls it. **Her canvas is never lost** (`drawing-app/drawapp/storage.py`): every finished stroke/clear rewrites `Pictures\Aiva and Saga Drawings\recovery\<author>-autosave.aivadraw`, a new window restores it (`DrawApp.restored`), and `_ensure` ADOPTS a window still up from before her restart (`launcher.find_running`: `<author>-window.json` + live pid + a `status` round trip whose pid and author must match) before launching. One owner per author per folder via an OS lock (`recovery\<author>.lock`); a second window autosaves to `-<pid>`. `DrawingBoard(launch=fake)` never looks for a real window unless `find=` is passed, and tests use `save_dir=tmp_path`, so a test can never adopt or overwrite his real canvas; drawapp's conftest points `USERPROFILE` at a temp home for children too. `Session.close()` drops `on_change` on purpose: a window↔session cycle left to the cyclic GC made PyQt5 fail-fast (0xC0000409) mid-suite. **Never route this through a terminal**: 2026-10-20 her task opened a PowerShell to run `python -c "…launch()…"` and he saw it every time. The child is `pythonw.exe` (CREATE_NO_WINDOW) with `--no-activate` (`WA_ShowWithoutActivating`), so his typing keeps its focus. Blocking work (`launch()` waits up to 20 s for the ready file) runs in `asyncio.to_thread` under a lock. A stroke is resent only if the app process is gone, never on a lost reply from a live app. **Launch or reuse from code** is `DrawingBoard.launch_or_reuse()` / `drawing.launch_or_reuse(author)` (blocking, speakable dict — a missing/unimportable `drawing-app/` included) or `await board.open()` on the loop. **Save from code** is `DrawingBoard.save_now(name)` / `drawing.save_drawing(author, name)` (blocking twin of `await board.save()`; the wire `save` command, never opens a window). Tasks save through the `save_drawing` tool in her `ToolBox` (pinned per sister by `test_a_task_saves_through_her_toolbox_in_her_own_name`). A process that booted before a drawing tool existed has no such tool for its tasks: 2026-10-20 one reported "no direct tool" and the request turned into a Claude Code delegate for a save. The fix is a restart, not new code. **Saving is never a keypress**: the same day a task without the tool pressed Ctrl+S into his focused window, so the `save_drawing` description and planner/runner prompts forbid Ctrl+S/`press_keys`. A pre-0.3 window answers `unsupported` and has no autosave. The reply says leave it open, never "close it": that would lose the drawing. **Her app is never the generic launcher's to guess**: the same day `launch_app("drawing-app")` put Aseprite up, so `tools.make_launch_app_handler(drawing)` sends any `is_drawing_app_request` name ("drawing app", "drawapp", "your canvas"; a named program like Aseprite/Paint never matches) to the board, and with no board refuses. The planner/runner prompts and both tool descriptions say the same. **Every canvas action is journaled** (2026-10-21, after their heart halves missed each other and only a screenshot showed it): `_guarded` → `_call` (speakable dict + outcome `ok|refused|failed|unknown`) → `_report` writes one JSON line through `DrawingJournal` to `aiva/logs/drawing-<author>-YYYYMMDD.jsonl`. A line holds the points as sent, bbox/start/end, color/width requested and effective (`_default_brush` = drawapp's `AUTHOR_COLORS`/`Brush`, the same table `commands.parse` uses), stroke_id, revision, window pid/port/adopted, and error/code. `close` is recorded too. The window and reply ride a `threading.local` (`_seen`) set in `_attach`/`_ensure`/`_stroke`/`_save`/`_open_saved`, so the spoken dicts are unchanged. `read_journal(day, authors, log_dir)` merges both sisters; on one window (same pid) the revision is the exact order. A journal that raises is suppressed. Tests get `JOURNAL_DIR` monkeypatched to tmp (autouse fixture in `test_drawing.py`), and `test_a_real_shared_canvas_can_be_redrawn_from_both_sisters_journals` proves the journal replays to the window's own autosave. Offscreen check without a window: `tests/test_drawing.py::test_a_real_canvas_opens_takes_strokes_and_closes`. On-screen clean check without a terminal: `..\.venv\Scripts\pythonw -m drawapp.verify --report %TEMP%\drawapp-verify.json`, run from `drawing-app/`, or with no terminal at all by opening `drawing-app/verify_drawing.pyw` (`open_file`/`os.startfile` → `pyw.exe`; it pins the app to the repo venv via `launcher.project_python()` because `pyw.exe` defaults to a Python without PyQt5) and reading `drawing-app/verify-report.json`.
- `aiva/modules/artist.py` — **her drawing hand: a whole picture while she keeps talking** (2026-10-27: asked for "more detail", she drew 50 `draw_stroke`s, one voice round trip each, in silence, until the 120 s turn cap cut her off, and the calls pushed 33 messages out of her context). `draw_picture(idea)` → `Artist.start` returns at once (`house_link.EnoughSaid`, so her one line is the only one) and spawns ONE side call (task model, label `draw`): `artist_messages` (`ARTIST_PROMPT_T` via `persona.named`, literal JSON braces) with `canvas_brief` of what's on the canvas, read by `DrawingBoard.on_canvas()` from the window's `recovery/<canvas>-autosave.aivadraw` (his and her sister's strokes too; never opens a window; NOT `canvas`, which is the board's canvas-name attribute). The reply is `{"strokes": [...], "said"}`: `points` lines, plus `ellipse` [cx, cy, rx, ry] with an optional `arc` that `expand` turns into points (models draw curves badly as raw points), clamped to the canvas, ≤150 strokes × 400 points. `parse_picture` is tolerant and drops bad shapes. They're drawn through the normal `DrawingBoard.stroke` (journaled) at `PACE_S`; three misses in a row stop it; `announce` (wired by `attach_voice`) says it's done, with no counts or coordinates. One picture at a time. `make_drawing_handlers(board, artist)` also sends her VOICE to draw_picture after `STROKE_RUN_MAX` (6) single strokes in `STROKE_RUN_S` (60 s); a task's ToolBox passes no `llm`, so its strokes never are. Not runner-allowed (a task would finish before the drawing). Tests: `tests/test_artist.py` (incl. a real offscreen canvas read back from its autosave).

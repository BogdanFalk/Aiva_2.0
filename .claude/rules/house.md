# The house: ear, floor, wire, link, sisters, kin, voices

- `aiva/house.py` — the house entry: mic input only, `MicController` (F9 mute = full **deafen**: zero-fills the STT path AND makes `wake()` ignore any wake with a preroll/verify, so the wake word can't wake it while muted — a sister's own preroll-less announces still can), two-stage wake word (N models via `modules/wake.py`, a verified transcript must contain a sister's name), idle/sleep, global hotkeys (F10 interrupt → holder only; `AIVA_HOTKEY_SISTER_TALK` flips private/audible; Ctrl+Alt+R restarts the sisters; Ctrl+Alt+X quits all), the speaker voiceprint + vocal affect computed ONCE per turn and forwarded, the emotion-hearing side-call started per turn in the background (never awaited before the grant; a late reading goes out via `Floor.late_affect` → queued turn or `proto.Affect`), the backchannel watch (with Flux: his mid-turn pauses → ONE sister gets a `proto.Backchannel`), the arbiter side-call, the dashboard launch, `aiva/house.db` (`house_transcript`, `house_events`, `house_kv`, the shared spend caps, house metrics) and `aiva/house_state.json` `{port, pid, boot_id}`.
- `aiva/modules/ear.py` — `MicController` / `ActivityWatcher` / `UserAudioTap` + device helpers, moved out of main.py. The mic gate is raised by the **Floor grant** (`set_speaking`), not by a Bot*SpeakingFrame — the speaking sister is in another process, and gating at grant is strictly earlier than any audio.
- `aiva/modules/house_stt.py` — `AIVA_STT=flux|nova` (default flux): `STTConfig.from_env` (pure; `AIVA_FLUX_EOT_THRESHOLD`/`_EOT_TIMEOUT_MS`/`_IDLE_CLOSE_S`), `build_stt`, `stage_params`. Flux (`flux-general-en`, 16 kHz, eager EOT OFF) drives the stage turn via `ExternalUserTurnStrategies` (no Silero, no smart-turn) and emits ONE final transcript per turn at EndOfTurn (no interims; `on_update` pokes the idle timer; `on_turn_info` feeds the backchanneler). Flux has no free keepalive, so `HouseFluxSTTService` closes its socket after the idle-close seconds without audio (ambient sleep drops audio) and reopens on the next frame — the wake pre-roll waits in the STT queue for the handshake. Empty finals are dropped by `ActivityWatcher` so a noise-only turn can't reject a wake. `nova` keeps the old nova-3 + Silero VAD + smart-turn v3 path.
- `aiva/modules/backchannel.py` — "mhm" while he talks. House: `Backchanneler` reads Flux's TurnInfo stream (`HouseFluxSTTService.on_turn_info`); a pause is the partial transcript not growing for `pause_s` while Flux holds the turn open; pure `decide()` gates it (min speech, cooldown, eot ceiling just under the end-of-turn threshold, no finished question, no dangling word/filler, probability), the house adds asleep/muted/unverified/floor-busy, and `target()` picks ONE sister. `proto.Backchannel(clip, voice)` is NOT a floor grant. The clip is deliberately not mic-gated (it would cut his words); `strip_echo()`/`strip_final` removes its echo from his final transcript at the word index where it fired. Sister: `ClipCache` synthesises `PHRASES` once per voice id + TTS model into `aiva/voice_cache/<slug>/` in the background; `house_link.BackchannelPlayer` (after the TTS) pushes `TTSStarted(append_to_context=False)`/audio/`TTSStopped`, `HouseLink.expect_backchannel()` swallows its speaking frames so the house never sees a turn, and `BackchannelVoice` adds the avatar `nod` cue (renderer protocol 8). Tests: `tests/test_backchannel.py`.
- `aiva/modules/house_stage.py` — `StageRouter` (consumes the stage aggregator's `LLMContextFrame`, hands the user's text to the Floor, drops mic audio) and `FloorUserMuteStrategy` (aggregator-level twin of the zero-fill, reads `floor.gate_active`).
- `aiva/modules/sisters.py` — PURE decisions: `NameSet`s (Aiva/Saga spellings + loose regexes), `addressee_decision` (names / group words / wake hint / follow-up / sticky / else arbiter), `exchange_should_continue` (sister rounds; caps, backoff, per-hour, quiet hours), `floor_decision` (priority `user > both_second > announce > sister > initiative`), `Turn`, the overheard-line and cue builders, `ARBITER_PROMPT` + tolerant `parse_arbiter_reply`. `ExchangeConfig.from_env()` reads the `AIVA_SISTER_*` caps.
- `aiva/modules/house_floor.py` — the `Floor`: the single grant authority. Queue → grant (holder, `gate_active` for audible turns, `turn` message) → `response start` ack → tools ±1 (re-run) → `response end` → release on `speaking off` | 1.5 s grace | immediately for private turns | 120 s cap | client death (+3 s gate tail) | interrupt. `said` → `house_transcript` row + `overheard` to the other sister + maybe a `sister` continuation (text-only in private mode). Presence: absent addressee → the present sister gets an `absent_cue`. Daily cap on sister-initiated chats. **His errands run to the end** (2026-10-14, he'd been playing middle man): "ask/tell/check with her" (`errand_to`) puts `errand_cue` on the turn (ask her NOW, opening with her name); a line routed away from an absent sister is kept in `_waiting` and picked up by the stand-in with `arrival_cue` when she joins within `ERRAND_TTL_S`; when an exchange rooted in an errand (`wants_report`) ends, the sent sister gets one `report` turn (`report_cue`, never continues). A call is a sentence opening with her name plus a comma/dash, a greeting or a question (`vocative`/`called_in`), after any filler like "yep"/"oh, hey" (`_OPENERS`) — "Saga's already here" is about her, not to her. A reply on his turn is labelled by `spoken_to`: her sister's slug when it opens with a call, `both` when it answers him and then calls her (overheard as "said to you and him"), else him; a `report` is always his. Knows nothing about sockets or pipecat; every timer is driven from `tick()`.
- `aiva/modules/house_proto.py` — the wire protocol (dataclasses + `encode/decode`, newline JSON): character→house `hello, response, tool, speaking, said, event, want_floor, mode, sleep, wake, pong, bye` (plus `control` and `say` from a `role=control` client, never a sister); house→character `welcome, reject, turn, overheard, affect, backchannel, interrupt, mode, state, absent, ping, shutdown`. Only `hello→welcome` is request/response; `turn` is the ONLY message that runs a sister's LLM.
- `aiva/modules/house_server.py` / `house_client.py` — `HouseServer` (one `ClientSession` per sister, sync `send` for the Floor, dup-slug ping-then-reject/replace, ping/pong liveness, `event` relay with `actor`/`event_id`, `state` broadcasts) and `HouseClient` (discover via the state file, `hello`, reader loop, 0.5→5 s backoff "waiting for the house", ≤5 queued `want_floor`s).
- `aiva/modules/house_link.py` — the sister-side processors: `HouseLink` (`run_turn` adds the lines to her context and pushes `LLMContextFrame` — no user aggregator/VAD/transcripts in her process; `add_overheard`; `interrupt` injects `InterruptionFrame` from below), `PrivacyGate` (stamps `skip_tts` on the LLM frames of a non-audible turn so the TTS forwards them untouched while the assistant aggregator still commits the text; fails closed; it is ALSO where a turn ends — straight after the LLM the frames are in true order, so a response that called a tool waits for the re-run and the first one without a tool sends `said` with the whole turn's text. Don't move that back to the assistant aggregator: after the ElevenLabs TTS the end frames are delayed or dropped and replies after a tool get lost), `MindWatcher` (`response start/end`, `tool ±1`), `UsageTracker` (+ `record("tts")`), `LipSyncTap`, and `BackchannelPlayer` (right after the TTS: plays a cached backchannel clip; `HouseLink.expect_backchannel` keeps its speaking frames from the house, so it is never a turn). `PrivacyGate` also strips Eleven v4 voice cues (`voice_tags.strip_tags`) from `said`, `take_said` and text-only replies.
- `aiva/modules/voice_tags.py` — Eleven v4 audio cues (pure). `performs_tags(tts_model)` (eleven_v4*/v3) decides both the TTS service in `main.py` (`ElevenLabsDialogueTTSService`, Text-to-Dialogue; `AIVA_TTS_STABILITY`) and the VOICE CUES persona block (`build_system_prompt(voice_cues=)`); a Flash voice gets neither, since it would read "[laughs]" aloud. `TAGS` is the short allowed list; `strip_tags` (only `[letters/spaces/'-]` cues, never the `[Saga said to him: "…"]` overheard format) keeps cues out of `said`, text-only replies and `Memory.save_new_messages`; her live context keeps them. Backchannel clips need nothing: the plain TTS endpoint accepts eleven_v4_turbo and clips are keyed by model. Tests: `tests/test_voice_tags.py`.
- `aiva/modules/character.py` — `Character.from_env(slug)`: everything that makes a sister herself (name, spellings, db/state paths, persona floor, voice, voice model (`llm_model`: `AIVA_LLM_MODEL`, which Saga shares unless `SAGA_LLM_MODEL` is set), avatar model, space dir, mood baseline, speaker seeding, wake model). Aiva's values reproduce the pre-house defaults byte-for-byte; Saga's come from the `SAGA_*` env block.
- `aiva/modules/kin.py` — what a sister knows about her sister **from experience**: `kin_observations` (did/made/read/said/likes, rolled up, strength-weighted, fading, vetoable) fed by the house-relayed public `event`s (tool use via `tools.bind_tool_hook`, autonomous acts, space finds, reading, tasks) and by her own diary digest (`sister_notes`); renders the "YOUR SISTER" block and answers `recall_about_sister` with **no LLM call** ("I haven't seen her do that" when unknown). Catch-up from `house_events` by a per-character cursor.
- `aiva/modules/speakers.py` — **open-set speaker recognition** ("who am I talking to?"). `SpeakerMemory` (voiceprints on `Memory.conn`, enroll/identify/re-centroid/rename/forget, dim+model_id guard), pure `decide()` (known / hold-sticky / unknown, thresholds in `SpeakerConfig`), `SpeakerRecognizer` (seed-primary silently, ask-when-unknown via `UNKNOWN_NOTE`, `enroll_current`, on-change "[Speaking now: X]" notes, lazy ONNX `embed_pcm` via **kaldi-native-fbank + onnxruntime**, offloaded to `asyncio.to_thread`). Model: CAM++ 192-d ONNX at `aiva/models/campplus_cn_en_common_200k.onnx` (gitignored). Wired in `main.py`: `UserAudioTap` after `mic` buffers the turn's 16k PCM; `ActivityWatcher.on_user_final` embeds it and injects the identity note before the LLM answers; `register_speaker_tools` (enroll/rename/forget); "WHO YOU'RE TALKING TO" persona block. Toggle `AIVA_SPEAKER_ID`; validate/tune with `tests/spike_speaker_id.py`. Tests: `tests/test_speakers.py`.
- `aiva/modules/paralinguistics.py` — **vocal affect** (how he SOUNDS, not who/what). Cheap numpy DSP on the same per-turn PCM the speaker tap grabs: RMS/pitch(f0)/pitch-variability/voiced-ratio → a coarse arousal (+weak valence) *relative to a running baseline* (`VocalAffect` holds the EMA baseline; pure `affect_from`/`extract_features`). Wired in `main.py`'s `_on_user_final`: emits a `voice_affect` event onto the experience bus so the self loop nudges her mood/tone to mirror yours (gentler when you sound tired, livelier when animated) — no note, no model, no new deps. Toggle `AIVA_VOICE_AFFECT`. The model-based second analyzer is `aiva/modules/emotion_hearing.py`: the same PCM as WAV → an OpenAI audio model (`AIVA_EMOTION_MODEL`, gpt-audio-mini; through `llm_kwargs`, `modalities=["text"]`, never `reasoning_effort`) → tolerant `parse_emotion` (fixed `VOCAB` + `BURSTS`, unknowns dropped, clamped) → `merge_affect` into the DSP dict as `affect["emotion"]`; the sister emits it with the `voice_affect` event and, only when confident, adds `emotion_note` ("[He sounds tired and a little amused — he laughed. ...]") to his turn (a late reading → his next turn, within `AIVA_EMOTION_NOTE_TTL_S`). Toggle `AIVA_EMOTION_HEARING`. Tests: `tests/test_paralinguistics.py`, `tests/test_emotion_hearing.py`.
